01 / Why we exist

The internet should feel more human.

SheSay is building a calmer kind of social experience: one that values context over virality, curiosity over outrage, and meaningful participation over endless scrolling.

We believe the best communities are built with care. That means giving people room to tell the whole story, tools to shape their own spaces, and a clear standard for how we treat one another.

02 / What guides us

Built around people, not metrics.

Every product decision starts with the kind of community we want to help make possible.

01

Make space

People deserve room to be nuanced, unfinished, joyful, and honest. We design for the full story.

02

Share generously

Useful knowledge travels further when it is easy to publish, discover, and build on together.

03

Protect trust

Safety is a product feature. Clear standards and thoughtful moderation help communities stay welcoming.

04 / How SheSay operates

A clear path from community value to revenue.

SheSay keeps the core community experience open while giving creators and readers optional ways to support useful work.

01

Creator tipping

Members can send optional tips to creators whose everyday wisdom, stories, and conversations they value.

02

Paid reports

Creators can publish premium reports and knowledge products with pricing shown before a member purchases.

03

Google Play settlement

Customer payments are processed through Google Play in-app purchases. Google Play remits SheSay LLC’s share after platform fees and applicable taxes.
