1. What are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to: remember your login status, save your language and theme preferences, and analyze app usage to optimize our services.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services (such as analytics tools) that may set their own cookies. Please refer to their privacy policies.

4. Your Choices

You can disable cookies in your device settings, but this may affect normal use of the App. Continued use constitutes acceptance of our use of cookies.
