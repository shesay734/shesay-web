Refund policy

How to request a refund and get help with an order.

Request a refund in the app

  1. Open the relevant order’s details page in the SheSay app.
  2. Tap the question mark (?) icon and choose “I want a refund”.
  3. Provide your reason and follow the prompts to submit your request.

If the order does not currently allow a refund request, or you cannot find the order, contact us through Help & feedback.

Request a refund through Help & feedback

Open Help & feedback on this website or in the app and send your request to our support team. You must log in first. On the website, you will be redirected to the login page and returned to your support conversation after signing in.

Please include:

  • Your order number (and payment receipt reference, if available).
  • The item or subscription purchased, purchase date, and amount and currency.
  • The reason for your refund request and a description of the issue.
  • Relevant receipt or error screenshots, if available. Remove unrelated personal or payment information.

Send the request from the account used for the purchase where possible. If you cannot access that account, explain this to support. Never send passwords, verification codes, or full payment card details.

Refunds and payment processing fees

You may request a refund at any time, provided that the credits have not been used. Please note that processing fees charged by third-party payment platforms—typically ranging from 3% to 10% of the total amount—may be deducted from your refund. All credits from a refunded top-up are removed from your balance.

After you submit

Submitting a request starts a review; it does not mean a refund has already been approved. Follow the order status in the app or continue your support conversation for updates and any requests for additional information. The team will confirm the outcome and, if approved, the refund destination and processing details for your order.
